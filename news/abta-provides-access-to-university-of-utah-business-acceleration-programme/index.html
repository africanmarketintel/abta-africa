ABTA Provides Access to University of Utah Business Acceleration Programme

ABTA members can now access the University of Utah's Masters of Business Creation programme with scholarship support up to $2,500 USD.

The MBC programme combines graduate education with structured business acceleration, running established cohorts for African entrepreneurs in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa.

Qualified entrepreneurs with active operations and clear scaling plans can contact abta@abta.africa for programme details and referral code access.
