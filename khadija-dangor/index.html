Khadija Dangor

Standard Bank Group

Advisory Board

Head of Operations, Liberty Financial Solutions (Standard Bank)

Khadija Dangor is a senior financial executive with over 20 years of global experience in global markets, risk management, internal controls and operations. She is currently in a senior management position within the Standard Bank Group and serves as a Non-Independent Director for an SPV. Her previous roles include positions at the National Bank of Abu Dhabi, JPMorgan Chase, and Investec Bank.

Khadija holds an Executive MBA and B.Comm Honours from the University of Johannesburg. She brings deep expertise in treasury, regulatory compliance, and strategic operations, supporting the African Business Trade Association’s mission to advance African trade and investment.

Connecting business, trade and investment across Africa.
