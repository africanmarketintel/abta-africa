Who is an ABTA member?

An ABTA member is anyone engaged or interested in doing business in Africa. Membership gives you access to the networks, investors and partners you need.

Become a member

As a member of the African Business Trade Association (ABTA), you have access to the networks, investors and partners you need to grow, scale and sustain your business. Together, members make an impact in their communities. ABTA was founded because that access is not equal. It is harder to reach if you look different, were born in another country or have no local networks. Your membership includes ABTA events, such as the Africa Trade Forum, where members and partners come together, and four African Market Intelligence (AMI) tools. News follows African markets and deals, Dealroom finds investors, buyers and partners, CRM tracks every deal and Mailroom emails your customers.

What does it mean to be an ABTA member?
  • Access to ABTA events, including the Africa Trade Forum.
  • AMI News Essential, to follow African business, markets and deals all year.
  • Dealroom, to find investors, buyers and partners for your deal. You stay confidential until ABTA makes the introduction.
  • AMI Mailroom, to email your customers from your own domain.
  • AMI CRM, to keep customers and contacts in one place, with notes, tasks and deals.
Who can become an ABTA member?

Anyone engaged or interested in doing business in Africa, from students and emerging professionals to SMEs and large corporates. Membership is open to organisations and individuals worldwide.

How does membership work?

Choose your membership tier, complete the application form and pay securely online, monthly or yearly. A member of the team then contacts you with instructions to access content and events.
