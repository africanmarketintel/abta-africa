Dr Masimba Zata

IFoA Council

Advisory Board

Chair of Trustees, Institute & Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA) Foundation

A seasoned actuarial leader in the insurance, technology and charity sectors, Masimba has a strong background in strategic leadership and Board governance.

As Chair of the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA) Foundation, he has been an active ambassador, driven fundraising strategy and built strategic partnerships. He has contributed to financial oversight and investment strategy on the Finance and Investment Committee, including pioneering the inclusion of ESG factors into the Investment Policy.

As a member of the IFoA Council, Masimba has contributed to significant governance reforms and represents the IFoA on the International Actuarial Association’s Global AI Task Force.

In his executive roles, Masimba has led Insurance Capital Efficiency projects and AI-driven Software Product Management, championed corporate governance, and advocated for diversity, equity and inclusion.

Masimba’s career began in Insurance and Pensions actuarial roles, and he continues to contribute to actuarial academia as a visiting lecturer at Queen Mary, University of London. He holds a Doctorate in Business Administration and is a Fellow of two professional bodies.
