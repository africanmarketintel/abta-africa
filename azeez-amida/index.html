Azeez Amida

Showtime Flag Football League

Advisory Committee, Business

Board Chair, Showtime Flag Football League | Fmr CEO IHS Towers Rwanda

Azeez Amida (The Growthpreneur) is a Nigerian-born business executive and author with two decades of professional experience cutting across six countries and 12 industries.

He was the CEO of IHS Towers, Rwanda and Pan African Towers, Nigeria’s largest indigenous Telecommunication infrastructure company. He also founded the Showtime Flag Football League – the largest coed flag football league in the world and Azeez Amida Foundation, an NGO focused on poverty alleviation, clean water, education and healthcare. He is also the author of the book ‘EPE Principle - Enter, Perform, Exit: understanding the opportunity cycle’.

Amida is an alumnus of the IE Business School, Spain, and a member of the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA).
