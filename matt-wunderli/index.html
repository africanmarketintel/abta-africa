Matt Wunderli

World Trade Center Utah

Advisory Board

Director of Investments, World Trade Centre Utah

Matt Wunderli is a trade strategist, tech entrepreneur, and Director at World Trade Center Utah, where he leads initiatives focused on foreign trade zones, global market expansion, and innovation policy. With an Executive MBA from the University of Cambridge, Matt blends academic rigor with real-world execution, advising companies and governments on cross-border growth and investment strategies. He has helped scale SaaS ventures, designed predictive economic frameworks, and worked at the intersection of geopolitics, technology, and trade.

A lifelong student of innovation and diplomacy, Matt is passionate about connecting emerging markets with capital, infrastructure, and ideas. He brings a cross-continental perspective rooted in partnership-building, practical growth, and bold economic thinking. His belief is simple: opportunity multiplies where ambition and access meet.
