Rachel Moré-Oshodi

Advisory Board

Managing Director & CEO, ARM-Harith Infrastructure Investment Ltd

Rachel is a seasoned leader in infrastructure finance and sustainable development, with over two decades of experience in transforming Africa’s energy sector. As the CEO of ARM-Harith Infrastructure Investments Ltd, she adeptly mobilizes capital for the development of innovative, climate-resilient infrastructure across Africa. Her leadership has been pivotal in driving over $7 billion worth of pioneering and award-winning projects, which have spurred economic growth and enhanced sustainable access to electricity, transport, and ICT services in emerging markets.

Rachel's career commenced at AES Corporation, where she developed essential energy assets, progressing through significant roles at global financial institutions, including the Inter-American Development Bank and the International Finance Corporation. Her strategic tenures at Rand Merchant Bank and TotalEnergies have highlighted her capability in leading investments that merge financial viability with profound social impact.

Dedicated to socio-economic empowerment, Rachel founded Ruwah, an innovative financial literacy platform that arms African youth with the skills necessary for financial independence, embodying her vision for a self-sufficient Africa.

A regular speaker at major global forums such as the UN General Assembly Climate Week and the World Economic Forum, Rachel champions infrastructure investment as a catalyst for inclusive growth. Equipped with an MBA from Darden and executive education from Harvard Business School, she continues to forge strategic, high-impact partnerships that propel sustainable development across Africa.

Connecting business, trade and investment across Africa.
