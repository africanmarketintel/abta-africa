AFSIC Conference and Expo 2025

Tuesday 14 October – Wednesday 15 October 2025 · 08:00–18:00 · Park Plaza Westminster, London

AFSIC – Investing in Africa 2025 is a leading investment event connecting investors, African business leaders and dealmakers. The African Business Trade Association is proud to partner with AFSIC to support investment-led growth across the continent. This results-focused platform features curated networking, project pitching and the AFSIC Deal Book, showcasing investor-ready opportunities from the African Investments Dashboard.
