Partner events

As part of our mission to support high-impact engagement on African trade, investment and enterprise, the African Business Trade Association partners with leading platforms and institutions across the continent and globally. This page showcases events where ABTA is actively involved – whether as a co-host, strategic content partner or supporting organisation. Each partnership reflects our commitment to convening decision-makers, sharing insights and strengthening networks that support Africa’s growth ambitions.

Partner event Past event

AFSIC Conference and Expo 2025

Tuesday 14 October – Wednesday 15 October 2025 · 08:00–18:00 · Park Plaza Westminster, London

AFSIC – Investing in Africa 2025 is a leading investment event connecting investors, African business leaders and dealmakers. The African Business Trade Association is proud to partner with AFSIC to support investment-led growth across the continent. This results-focused platform features curated networking, project pitching and the AFSIC Deal Book, showcasing investor-ready opportunities from the African Investments Dashboard.
