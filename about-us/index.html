About ABTA

The African Business Trade Association (ABTA) is a London-based membership organisation connecting businesses, investors and institutions with African markets. ABTA operates the African Market Intelligence (AMI) platform providing market and sector insight, the Dealroom for capital introductions, and curated business matching; and practical support for market entry and expansion to businesses setting up, operating or expanding across the continent. The Africa Trade Forum is ABTA’s annual flagship event.
