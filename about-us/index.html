About ABTA

The African Business Trade Association (ABTA) gives you access to the networks, investors and partners you need to grow, scale and sustain your business. Together, members make an impact in their communities.

You meet members and partners at ABTA events, including the Africa Trade Forum each year. Four African Market Intelligence (AMI) tools are also included in membership. With them, you can:

  • follow African business, markets and deals with AMI News
  • email your customers with AMI Mailroom
  • know who to follow up today and track every deal from new to won with AMI CRM
  • find investors, buyers and partners for a deal with AMI Dealroom

Our story

Why ABTA exists

Access to resources is not equal. It is harder to reach the networks, investors and partners you need if you look different, were born in another country or have no local networks.

ABTA was founded so that this generation and the next do not struggle to reach these resources.

Who ABTA serves

Growing businesses

Growing businesses use ABTA for access to the resources, tools and people they need to grow and scale.

Established businesses

Established businesses use ABTA for access to networks to enter new markets, build new partnerships, reach investors for deals and raise their profile.
