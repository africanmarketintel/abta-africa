Growth Programmes

ABTA members have access to the University of Utah's Masters of Business Creation (MBC) programme with scholarship support up to $2,500 USD. The programme combines graduate education with business acceleration for entrepreneurs scaling companies in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa.

The MBC programme is designed for entrepreneurs with active operations, clear growth plans and commitment to structured scaling.

Frequently asked questions

Who is ABTA membership for?

Anyone engaged in African trade and investment, from students and emerging professionals to SMEs and large corporates.

What benefits do members receive?

AMI News Essential, Dealroom, AMI Mailroom and AMI CRM for a full year at less than the cost of buying them separately. Members also have access to the Growth Programmes.

Can I upgrade my membership later?

Yes. Simply complete the application for the higher tier and pay the difference.

Do members receive consultancy services?

No. Consultancy is offered separately as a bespoke service. Speak to our team to find out more.

Is membership open internationally?

Yes. ABTA membership is open to organisations and individuals worldwide. All digital publications and events are accessible online globally.

What is AMI News?

AMI News is ABTA's African Market Intelligence service, providing sector intelligence, policy updates and market insights across African markets. Every membership tier includes AMI News Essential for the full year.

What happens after I join?

You will receive an immediate on-screen confirmation, and a member of our team will contact you shortly with instructions to access content and events.
